Æramyd Plot

Measured sketches for site work.

See lengths live as you draw, in metric or imperial. Perfect lines, arcs and shapes, with presets for construction, electrical, solar and civil jobs.

Open Æramyd Plot(opens in a new tab) Freemium · No ads · No account · Works offline

Features

Live length

Every line shows its length while you draw, in metric or imperial units.

Perfect shapes

Lines, arcs, circles, squares, rectangles and triangles snap clean on lift.

Trade presets

Construction, electrical, solar and civil presets with symbols, tools, layers and line styles.

Rooms and tools

Closed loops become rooms automatically. Dimension, measure, offset and trim tools.

Photo calibration

Set a known length on a photo and sketch to scale over it.

PDF and CSV export

Share a clean PDF plan or a CSV list of measurements.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Æramyd Plot is freemium with no ads. The core features are free, and Pro is a one-time unlock with no subscription.

Who is it for?

Anyone doing site jobs: builders, electricians, solar installers, civil and maintenance crews, and homeowners planning a project.

Does it work with a finger?

Yes. It works with a finger on phones, and with Apple Pencil or other tablet pens on tablets.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit it works without internet. For the best experience, install it on your home screen.

How do I install it?

iPhone and iPad: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app. Windows, Mac and Chromebook: open it in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon in the address bar.
