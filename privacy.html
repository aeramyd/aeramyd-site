Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: Æramyd does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts.
  • Your light or dark mode choice is saved only in your own browser.
  • The Inter font is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a normal web request from your browser.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare may process basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.

Æramyd apps

  • Your drawings and files stay on your device. They are not uploaded to us.
  • Photos you add are used only on your device, inside the app.
  • Optional support payments are handled by PayPal under PayPal's own privacy policy. Æramyd does not see or store your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
